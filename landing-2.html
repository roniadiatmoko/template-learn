    How to join, step by step

    The whole process is online. Most applicants finish the first three steps in one evening.

    1. Create your account

      Sign up with your email and confirm it. One account is used for admission, then for your student portal.

    2. Choose your study programs

      Pick up to two programs. You can compare entry requirements and tuition before you decide.

    3. Upload your documents

      Add your school certificate or transcript, ID, and a recent photo. Files are checked within three working days.

    4. Take the entrance test

      Sit the computer-based test online or at one of our campus test centers. Practice questions are free.

    5. Check results and enroll

      See your result online, pay the first tuition installment, and collect your student card.
